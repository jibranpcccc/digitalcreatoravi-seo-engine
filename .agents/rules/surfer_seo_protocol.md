# Surfer SEO Content Auditor & SERP Optimization Standard

When drafting or optimizing new articles:
1. SERP COMPETITOR ANALYSIS: Inspect top ranking pages for the target keyword.
2. NLP KEYWORD MATRIX: Extract 15 essential NLP terms (Primary, Secondary, LSI) with recommended frequency counts.
3. CONTENT OUTLINE: Build H1, H2, H3 outline covering competitor content gaps.
4. ARTICLE AUDIT SCORE: Target 100/100 on-page SEO score with featured snippet box (45-60 words), schema JSON-LD, and zero fluff.
5. OPTIMIZED CONTENT GENERATION: Incorporate all target NLP terms naturally.
